[h1]Better Key Bindings[/h1]
Bind Ctrl + E, Shift + Mouse 4, the middle mouse button or the mouse wheel to any action, without the plain key triggering as well.

[h2]What's new in 1.1.0[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Mouse wheel[/b]: wheel up and wheel down can be bound, alone or with modifiers (Ctrl + wheel up). One notch triggers once, and a notch that goes to a binding does not also scroll.
[*]While a binding waits for a key, what you press goes only to that binding: Space does not press a button and the wheel does not scroll the list.
[*]A press is decided once, as it goes down: the exact combination first, else the bindings with the most of the held modifiers, else the game gets it untouched.
[*]Rotate, flip and reset only take a key while you drag an item, so elsewhere the wheel still scrolls.
[/list]

[h2]What it adds[/h2]
[list]
[*]Every key binding in the options takes [b]Ctrl, Shift and Alt combinations[/b]. Hold the modifiers and press the key.
[*][b]Middle mouse button, mouse buttons 4 and 5 and the mouse wheel[/b] can be bound like any key, with or without modifiers.
[*][b]No double triggers[/b]: with E on one action and Ctrl + E on another, Ctrl + E runs only the second.
[*][b]Conflict prompt[/b]: choosing a key that is already in use asks whether to unbind the other binding.
[*]The key the game fixes for [b]precise placement[/b] (Shift) becomes a binding.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Changes wait for [b]Apply[/b], like the game's own key choices.
[*]Bindings that other mods add to the options take combinations too.
[*]Text in all eleven languages of the game.
[*]All of its UI uses the game's own assets, so texture packs restyle it too.
[*]Needs no special permissions.
[*]Built for game version 0.46D.
[/list]

[h1]按键绑定增强[/h1]
把 Ctrl + E、Shift + 鼠标按键 4、鼠标中键或鼠标滚轮绑定到任何操作，而且不会顺带触发单键绑定。

[h2]1.1.0 新内容[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]鼠标滚轮[/b]：滚轮向上、向下可以单独绑定，也可以搭配修饰键（Ctrl + 滚轮向上）。滚一格只触发一次，交给绑定的那一格不会同时滚动。
[*]绑定等待按键时，你按下的键只交给这个绑定：空格不会按下按钮，滚轮也不会滚动列表。
[*]一次按下只在按下时判定一次：先找完全一致的组合，再找修饰键保留最多的绑定，都没有就原样交给游戏。
[*]旋转、翻转和复位只在拖动物品时占用按键，其他时候滚轮照常滚动。
[/list]

[h2]新增功能[/h2]
[list]
[*]选项中的每个按键绑定都支持 [b]Ctrl、Shift、Alt 组合键[/b]：按住修饰键再按主键即可。
[*][b]鼠标中键、鼠标按键 4 和 5、鼠标滚轮[/b]可以像普通按键一样绑定，也可以搭配修饰键。
[*][b]不会误触发[/b]：E 绑定一个操作、Ctrl + E 绑定另一个操作时，按 Ctrl + E 只会执行后者。
[*][b]冲突提示[/b]：选择已被占用的按键时，会询问是否解除原有绑定。
[*]游戏写死的[b]精确放置[/b]键（Shift）变成可绑定项。
[/list]

[h2]说明[/h2]
[list]
[*]和游戏自带的按键设置一样，点 [b]应用[/b] 后才会生效。
[*]其他模组添加到选项里的按键绑定也支持组合键。
[*]支持游戏的全部十一种语言。
[*]所有界面都使用游戏自带的 UI 资源，材质包也会一并生效。
[*]不需要任何特殊权限。
[*]适用于游戏版本 0.46D。
[/list]
